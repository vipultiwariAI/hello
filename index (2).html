Built by teachers, not a franchise

Vipul Education started in 2013 in a single rented room with fourteen students and a blackboard. Today it runs from a full campus in Aliganj, but the promise hasn't changed: every student gets taught, not just lectured to.

Batches are capped so every child gets time with a teacher, not just a seat in a hall. Our faculty are subject specialists — the person teaching Chemistry has taught nothing else for over a decade.

We track every student's weak topics week over week, so parents get a real answer to "how is my child doing," not a generic report card.
